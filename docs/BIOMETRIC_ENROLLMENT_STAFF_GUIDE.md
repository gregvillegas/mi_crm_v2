# MI CRM — Biometric Sign-In: Staff Enrollment Guide

**Sign in with your fingerprint or face — no password to type.**

> This 1-page guide shows how to register your device so you can log in to the
> CRM using Touch ID, Face ID, Windows Hello, or your phone's fingerprint.
> It's optional, quick (about 1 minute), and your password still works as a backup.

---

## Before you start

- You need a device with a biometric sensor: **iPhone/iPad** (Face ID/Touch ID),
  **Mac** (Touch ID), **Windows 10/11 PC** (Windows Hello), or **Android phone**
  (fingerprint/face).
- Use a **supported browser**: Safari, Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, or Firefox.
- Access the CRM at **https://micrm.microimageph.com** (must be the real site, over HTTPS).

---

## How to enroll (one time)

1. **Log in** to the CRM the normal way (username + password).
2. Click your **name/avatar** at the top-right → **My Profile**.
3. Open the **Security** section.
4. Under **Biometric / Passkey**, click **“Register this device.”**
5. Your device will prompt you:
   - **iPhone/iPad/Mac:** approve with **Face ID / Touch ID**.
   - **Windows:** approve with **Windows Hello** (fingerprint, face, or PIN).
   - **Android:** approve with your **fingerprint / face unlock**.
6. Done ✅ — you'll see the device listed as registered.

*Tip: Give the device a name you'll recognize (e.g. "J— Office Laptop") if asked.*

---

## How to sign in with biometrics (every time after)

1. Go to **https://micrm.microimageph.com** and reach the login page.
2. Click **“Sign in with biometrics / passkey.”**
3. Confirm with your **fingerprint / face** when your device asks.
4. You're in — no password typing needed on that device.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the company store my fingerprint or face?**
**No.** Your fingerprint/face never leaves your device. The CRM only stores a
secure digital key — it cannot see or reconstruct your biometric.

**What if I'm on a different computer?**
Just log in with your **username + password** as usual. Biometrics is tied to the
specific device(s) you register. You can register more than one device.

**What if I get a new phone/laptop or lose my device?**
Log in with your **password**, go to **Profile → Security**, and **register the new
device**. If needed, use your **recovery codes** to get in. Old devices can be removed
from the same page.

**Do I still need my password?**
Keep it. Password (and recovery codes) remain your backup and are required on any
device you haven't enrolled.

**It didn't prompt for my fingerprint — why?**
Make sure (a) you're on **https://micrm.microimageph.com**, (b) your device biometric
is set up in the OS (e.g. Windows Hello / Face ID enabled), and (c) you're using a
supported browser. If it still fails, contact IT.

---

## Need help?

Contact **IT / System Administrator** for assistance with enrollment or if you're
locked out. Do not share your recovery codes with anyone.
