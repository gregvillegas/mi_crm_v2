# Lead Generation Guide

This guide explains how to use the **Lead Generation** module in the CRM, from capturing new leads to qualifying, converting, or closing them as lost.

## Purpose

The Lead Generation module helps teams:

- capture and organize potential customers
- prioritize follow-up using lead scoring
- track lead activities and next actions
- mark leads as lost when opportunities do not move forward
- convert qualified leads into customers
- analyze lead source performance and conversion trends

## Accessing Lead Generation

- Open the **Leads** module from the main navigation bar.
- The default screen is the **Lead Generation Dashboard**.

## Lead Generation Dashboard

The dashboard gives a quick snapshot of the current lead pipeline.

### Summary Cards

The top cards show:

- **Total Leads**: all leads in the module
- **Lost Leads**: leads already marked as lost
- **Hot Leads**: high-priority or high-score leads
- **Conversion**: leads already converted to customers
- **Conversion Rate**: current conversion percentage

Each card can also serve as a shortcut to filtered lead lists.

### Quick Actions

Depending on your role, the dashboard provides shortcuts such as:

- **Add New Lead**
- **My Leads**
- **Hot Leads**
- **Analytics**

### Follow-up Required

This section highlights leads with pending next follow-up dates.

Use it to:

- identify overdue or upcoming follow-ups
- focus first on active opportunities
- open related lead records for action

### Top Performing Lead Sources

This section helps managers and users compare source performance.

It shows:

- source name
- source type
- total leads
- conversions
- conversion rate

### Recent Activity

This section shows the latest lead actions, such as:

- new lead creation
- follow-up logging
- demos
- email requests
- status changes
- leads marked as lost

- phone number
- job title

- timeline

- requirements

## Lead Scoring

The system automatically calculates a lead score using available profile and engagement data.

### Score Interpretation

- **80+**: Hot
- **60+**: Warm / ready
- **Below 60**: Cold

### Automatic Qualification

Leads with a score of **70 or higher** are automatically marked as **Qualified**.

Important:

- the system updates the **qualified flag**
- the system does **not** automatically force every lead status to jump forward
- a lead can therefore be qualified even if the visible status still needs manual progression

## Lead Detail Page

The lead detail page is the main working screen for one lead.

### What You Can See

- contact information
- company information
- lead details
- lead score
- activity timeline
- current status, priority, and assigned salesperson

### Main Actions

Depending on the lead state and your permissions, you may see:

- **Edit Lead**
- **Log Activity**
- **Convert to Customer**
- **Mark as Lost**

## Logging Lead Activity

Use **Log Activity** to record progress on a lead.

Examples:

- follow-up
- email sent
- call made
- meeting/demo
- proposal request
- status update

Why this matters:

- builds the lead timeline
- keeps history visible for the team
- helps managers monitor progress
- supports follow-up planning

## Follow-up Management

Follow-up is controlled through activity logging and next follow-up dates.

Best practice:

- always enter the next expected action
- keep notes short and clear
- update the lead after every meaningful interaction

Leads with pending follow-up dates appear in the dashboard’s **Follow-up Required** section.

## Marking a Lead as Lost

When an opportunity is no longer moving forward, use **Mark as Lost**.

### How to Mark a Lead as Lost

1. Open the lead detail page.
2. Click **Mark as Lost**.
3. Select a loss reason.
4. Add optional notes.
5. Confirm the action.

### What Happens

- the lead status changes to **Lost**
- the lead is removed from normal active conversion flow
- a lead activity entry is added for audit history

### Typical Reasons

- not interested
- budget constraints
- chose competitor
- timing not right
- no response
- other

## Converting a Lead to Customer

When a lead is ready to become an actual customer, use **Convert to Customer**.

### When Conversion Is Available

Conversion is available when the lead is eligible, such as:

- officially qualified
- already in later stages like proposal or negotiation
- not already converted
- not marked lost

### How to Convert

1. Open the lead detail page.
2. Click **Convert to Customer**.
3. Review the lead summary.
4. Enter:
   - **Conversion Value**
   - optional **Notes**
5. Optionally enable:
   - **Create Sales Funnel Entry**
   - choose the initial **Sales Funnel Stage**
6. Confirm conversion.

### What Happens During Conversion

- a new customer record is created
- the lead status changes to **Converted**
- the converted customer is linked back to the lead
- a conversion tracking record is saved
- an activity entry is logged
- an optional sales funnel entry can be created

### Important Notes

- conversion is not intended to be reversed automatically
- the lead remains available for reporting and historical reference
- if you want to create a sales funnel entry, the lead should be assigned to a salesperson first

## Sales Funnel Option During Conversion

When converting a lead, users can optionally create a sales funnel record immediately.

This is useful when:

- the lead already represents an active opportunity
- the team wants to continue deal tracking right away
- a quotation or negotiation is expected after conversion

The initial funnel stage can be selected during the conversion form.

## Importing Leads by CSV

Bulk import is available from **Leads → Import**.

### Recommended Process

1. Download the sample import template.
2. Fill in the required fields using the correct column order.
3. Upload the CSV file.
4. Choose whether to auto-calculate lead scores during import.
5. Review the import results.

### Notes

- assignment options may be filtered by role
- invalid rows should be corrected and re-imported
- imported leads can immediately appear in scoring and dashboard counts

## Recommended Daily Workflow

For salespeople:

1. Open the **Lead Generation Dashboard**
2. Review **Follow-up Required**
3. Check **Hot Leads**
4. Log calls, emails, demos, and notes
5. Mark dead opportunities as **Lost**
6. Convert qualified opportunities to **Customer**

For managers:

1. Monitor dashboard counts
2. Review **Top Performing Lead Sources**
3. Check **Recent Activity**
4. Track lost leads and conversions
5. Review whether high-score leads are being progressed properly

## Best Practices

- keep lead information complete and updated
- log every meaningful interaction
- assign ownership clearly
- use loss reasons consistently
- convert leads only when they are ready to enter customer management
- use the sales funnel option during conversion if opportunity tracking should continue immediately

## Troubleshooting Tips

### A lead has a high score but is not converting properly

- confirm the lead is already marked qualified
- refresh the lead after recalculation or recent edits
- check that the lead is not already lost or converted

### Convert to Customer is not visible

- the lead may not yet be eligible for conversion
- the lead may already be converted
- the lead may already be marked lost

### Sales funnel entry cannot be created during conversion

- confirm the lead has an assigned salesperson
- then retry conversion with the sales funnel option enabled
