Safest Import Path: This importer adds or updates related additional contacts only. It does not create new customers, replace customer IDs, or modify links used by proposals, activities, tickets, campaigns, or lead conversions.
CSV Format Instructions:
  • Required columns: `customer_email`, `contact_name`
  • Optional columns: `contact_position`, `contact_email`, `contact_phone`, `is_primary`
  • Customer match: `customer_email` must match an existing customer email exactly
  • Updates: Existing contacts are matched first by `contact_email`, then by contact name for the same customer
  • Limit: Up to 4 additional contacts per customer
  • Primary flag: Use `Yes`/`No`, `True`/`False`, or `1`/`0`
