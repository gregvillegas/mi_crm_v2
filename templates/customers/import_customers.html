Import Customers from CSV

CSV Format Instructions:
  • Required columns: Company Name, Contact Person Name, Email
  • Optional columns: Phone Number, Address, Industry, Territory, Millionaire Status, Active Status, Salesperson Username
  • Industry values: Technology, Finance & Banking, Healthcare, Manufacturing, etc.
  • Territory values: Makati City, Ortigas, Pasig City, Alabang, etc.
  • Millionaire Status: Exported for reporting but automatically recalculated by the customer sync command.
  • Active Status: Use "Yes"/"No" or "True"/"False" for the manual active flag.
  • Salesperson Username: Must exist and have role 'salesperson' (leave blank if none)
  • Created/Updated At: Will be auto-generated (can be left blank)
  • Email addresses must be unique across all customers
  • Download sample CSV for the complete format reference
